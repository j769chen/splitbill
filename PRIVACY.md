Splitfox is a bill-splitting app. It is published by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we", "us"). You can reach us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

## What we collect

**When you create an account**
- Your name (the display name you type).
- Your email address.
- Your password. It is sent to our backend provider's sign-in service, which stores it hashed. The app never stores your password on your phone.

**What you add to the app**
- Groups you create or join, and their members.
- Expenses (amount, currency, description, category, date, who paid and how it is split).
- Payments you record to settle up (amount, currency, optional note, who paid whom).
- Friends ("contacts") you connect with, and contact requests you send or receive.
- When you type another person's email address (to add them to a group or send a contact request), it is sent to our backend so it can look up their account. It is not stored.

**On your phone**
- Your sign-in session, kept in the phone's secure storage (backed by the Android Keystore).
- Your settings: light or dark theme, display currency and notification switches. These are stored on the phone only, and may be included in Android's device backup if you have that turned on.
- A copy of public exchange rates, so balances can be shown in your chosen currency.

## What we do not collect

Splitfox has no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no push notifications. It does not ask for your location, your phone's contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files, and it does not read advertising or device identifiers. We do not sell your data.

## Who can see your data

- **People in a group with you** can see your name, that you are a member, every expense and payment in that group (amounts, descriptions, notes, who paid, how it was split), and each member's balance.
- **Your contacts, and anyone you have a one-to-one expense or payment with,** can see your name and the expenses and payments between the two of you.
- **If you leave a group,** the expenses and payments you already added stay in that group, with your name, for the people who are still in it.
- **Nobody sees your email address through the app.** It is not shown to other users.
- **Anyone with a Splitfox account can check whether an email address has an account.** If they then send a contact request to it, they will see the name on that account.
- **A group member can add any registered person to a group without that person accepting first.** The person added can then see that group's data, and can leave once they have no unsettled balance.

## Service providers

- **Supabase** hosts our database and sign-in service. Your account details and everything you add to the app are stored there. Like any hosting provider, it receives your device's IP address and standard request details when the app connects.
- **An exchange-rate service** (open.er-api.com). The app downloads public exchange rates from it so balances can be shown in other currencies. The request contains no account information, but, as with any web request, the service can see the device's IP address.

All connections from the app are encrypted (HTTPS).

## How long we keep data

We keep your data until it is deleted. You can delete individual expenses and payments in the app. When the last member leaves a group, the group and its history are deleted. When you delete your account, some things are removed and some stay in shared records, shown as "Deleted user", as described in the next section.

## Your choices and deleting your account

- You can change your name in the app (Account, Edit profile).
- You can delete expenses and payments, and leave a group once you have no unsettled balance.
- **You can delete your account in the app** (Account, Delete account). If you cannot use the app, email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete it. We will reply to confirm it is you, and we will delete your account within [NUMBER OF DAYS] days of confirming.
- **What is removed when an account is deleted:** your email address, your password and your sign-in; your name and picture; your contact requests, sent and received; expenses and payments dated in the future that involve you; and any repeating expense you are part of stops. You are removed from other people's friends lists, unless money is still owed between you and that person: then they keep you listed as "Deleted user" and the one-to-one ledger stays. A group with no other person with an active account left in it is deleted; a group that still has other active members stays, and if you created it, it is handed to one of them. People can no longer find you by email. We also delete the sign-in log entries our sign-in service keeps for your account, where we are able to.
- **What stays:** the expenses and payments you took part in stay for the other people in those groups and for your friends, so that nobody else's balance changes. Your name on them is shown as "Deleted user" and nothing links them to your email address any more. The amounts, descriptions, dates and notes that you typed stay too. Balances with individual friends stay on their ledger. If you still owed money, or were owed money, in a group, you stay listed in it as "Deleted user" so the others can still settle with you and balances do not change.
- **Please settle up first if you can.** Deleting an account cannot be undone, and the balances people have with you stay owed after it is deleted. We are happy to talk you through the options before we delete anything.
- Our hosting provider may keep routine backups for a short time, on a schedule we do not control.

## Children

Splitfox is not directed at children and is not intended for people under 13 (or the higher minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will remove it.
