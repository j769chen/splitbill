# Splitfox: Google Play store listing draft

Copy for the Play Console (Grow, Store presence, Main store listing). Draft only: the account owner reviews and edits before pasting. Written against the Play metadata policy: no other app or brand names, no ranking or price claims that are not true, no emoji spam, no ALL CAPS.

## App name (30 characters max)

```
Splitfox: Split Bills
```

21 characters. Play's metadata policy does not allow price or promotional words like "Free" in the title, so the free positioning lives in the descriptions.

## Short description (80 characters max)

```
Split bills with friends and groups. Free, with no ads and no limits.
```

69 characters.

## Full description (4,000 characters max)

```
Splitfox makes it easy to share costs with the people you live, travel and hang out with. Add what you spent, choose how to split it, and Splitfox keeps track of who owes whom.

Splitfox is free. There are no daily limits on how many expenses you can add, no ads, and no paid Pro tier hiding the useful parts.

Split it your way
- Split equally, by exact amounts or by percentages
- Add an expense to a group or to a single friend
- Edit an expense if you got it wrong

Know who owes what
- See your balance with every friend and every group at a glance
- Simplify debts so a group settles up in the fewest possible payments
- Record a payment when someone settles up, and balances update right away

Groups and friends
- Make a group for a trip, a household or a night out and add people by their email (they need a Splitfox account)
- Keep a list of friends for the one-off expenses that do not belong to a group
- An activity feed shows what changed and when

Made for every day
- Updates show up live for everyone in a group
- View balances in the currency you prefer
- Light and dark themes

Sign up with your email address and start splitting in a minute. Splitfox is made by [DEVELOPER NAME].
```

- **Alerts.** The notification switches in Settings only save a local preference today and nothing sends push notifications yet, so the listing does not mention alerts. Add it when that ships.
- **Developer name.** The last line has the placeholder "[DEVELOPER NAME]". Replace it with the public developer name the Play Console shows for the account Splitfox is published under (the account owner chose the existing Overture Interactive account), and check it matches exactly.
- **Features.** Everything listed exists in the app at this commit. If the recurring and future-dated transactions work lands later, add a line for it in the update that ships it.
- **Hosted pages (live):** privacy policy https://splitfox.overtureinteractive.com/privacy (paste into Console, Policy and programs, App content, Privacy policy) and account deletion https://splitfox.overtureinteractive.com/delete-account (the delete account URL in the Data safety form).
